Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 1, 2026

OpenBin.ai (“OpenBin,” “we,” “our”) operates the services at openbin.ai, app.openbin.ai, and openapk.ai (collectively, the “Services”). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can control it. OpenBin is bring-your-own-key by design: your binary, your decompiled tree, your reports, your LLM provider key - we hold the workspace, you hold the intelligence.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account through our identity provider (Keycloak), we store your email address and chosen display name. We do not collect a date of birth, address, phone number, or government identifier.
  • LLM provider credentials. If you choose to use AI features, you paste your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or AWS Bedrock API key. We encrypt these at rest with a key-encryption-key (KEK) the operator of the instance controls. Decrypted plaintext keys never leave method scope and are not cached or logged.
  • Project content. Binaries you upload, decompiled output, screenshots, annotations, reports, and audit entries. Stored in Postgres + S3 operated by us.
  • Usage and audit logs. Per-call token counts, cost estimates, and high-level action logs (e.g., “project X created”) used to power your Usage page and respond to abuse reports.
  • Cookies and session data. A session cookie issued by Keycloak for sign-in. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

2. What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics or tracker scripts.
  • No telemetry pings or behavior heatmaps.
  • We do not proxy your LLM calls. Prompts and responses go directly between your browser/server and the provider you configured; we never see them in transit.
  • We do not sell, share, or train models on your project content.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Operate the Services (authentication, project workspaces, AI features you invoke).
  • Send transactional email related to your account (see §4).
  • Respond to abuse reports and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and lawful process.

4. Email

We send transactional email only. There is no marketing email, no newsletter, no promotional broadcast list. Outbound mail is sent through Amazon SES from a verified domain identity (openapk.ai / openbin.ai) with DKIM, SPF, and DMARC configured.

Categories of email you may receive:

  • Account verification - one message when you sign up, containing a one-time verification link.
  • Password reset - only when you initiate it yourself by clicking “forgot password.”
  • Security notifications - new sign-in from an unfamiliar device, password changed, MFA enrolled.
  • Abuse-report responses - if you flag content or are the subject of a flag, we may email you about the resolution.

Every account email contains an Auto-Submitted: auto-generated header and a List-Unsubscribe header per RFC 8058. To stop receiving all email from us, delete your account from the Settings page or email husam@openbin.ai with the subject “Delete my account.” Hard bounces and spam complaints are honored automatically: we suppress the affected address and stop further sends without requiring action from you.

5. Sharing with third parties

  • LLM providers. When you run an analysis with AI assistance, the relevant prompts and source/strings are sent to the LLM provider you configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS Bedrock). Their privacy policies apply to that traffic.
  • Amazon Web Services. We use AWS for compute (ECS), storage (S3), database (RDS Aurora), identity (Keycloak self-hosted), email (SES), and DNS (Route 53). AWS acts as our processor under standard data-processing terms.
  • Law enforcement. We may disclose information if compelled by lawful process. We will tell you unless legally prohibited.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Community publishing

Reports you publish to the community are public to anyone on the internet. Drafts and unpublished projects are private to you. Your published reports are attributed by your chosen display name; we never display your raw email publicly - only a Gravatar identifier (an MD5 hash of your email) is shown to anonymous readers. See our Terms of Service for what content is permitted.

7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete a project at any time from the Projects page; that removes its binary, decompiled output, screenshots, and report. To delete your entire account, use Settings → Delete account, or email husam@openbin.ai. Deletion removes your account, credentials, projects, drafts, and audit log within 30 days. Community reports already published may be retained as already-public content; we will unpublish on request and replace your display name with “deleted user.”

8. Security

We encrypt provider credentials with AES-256-GCM using a master key we control. All traffic is over TLS 1.2+. Workloads run in a private VPC. Database backups are encrypted with AWS KMS. We do not store passwords (Keycloak hashes them with Argon2id).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email husam@openbin.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

OpenBin is operated from the United States. If you use the Services from outside the United States, you understand and consent to the transfer of your information to the United States.

12. Changes

We may update this notice as the product evolves. Material changes will be announced on this page and, where required, by email. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
